## Lessons fed to the writer
- Open with a concrete physical action and stake in present tense (enters, leaves car open, crosses with 10 mil pavos); avoid vague 'qué podía hacer él' setups.
- Prefer H3 hooks: two short sentences, setup then twist tease ('Ella pidió una cosa.' / 'Adivina quién se cuela.').
- Use V4 predictions or V3 Rico tips with a specific Leonida joke; drop V1 numeric scores, they consistently flop.
- Prioritize IlloJuan and Rubius clips; avoid Auronplay until a new angle is tested.
- Use caption style A and post at 10 AM, 7 AM or 6 PM PT; avoid B captions and 12 PM or 3 PM slots.
